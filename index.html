How to use the Name Resolver

The Name Resolver turns plain text into War Era links. Paste the result into the news editor and every recognized name becomes a clickable, hoverable entity that reveals its profile — a country, region, alliance, party, military unit, or player.

What you can resolve

Country, region, alliance, party, military unit (MU), and player usernames.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your draft into the text field.
  2. Press Resolve (or Ctrl+Enter).
  3. Copy the output and paste it into the War Era news editor.

Modes

  • Smart — uses AI to filter out accidental matches. Slower, but fewer false positives.
  • Naive — links every known name instantly. Faster, but may over-match.
